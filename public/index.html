A few things to know

Good to know.

Do I need an account?+

No. Yutaka works offline with locally stored data. A sync account is only needed if you connect your own self-hosted backend.

Which platforms are supported?+

Yutaka is available for Android, Windows, Linux, and macOS. Open Get Yutaka and download your installer directly. Alternative architectures and archive formats are available under Other downloads.

How does multi-device sync work?+

Deploy your own Cloudflare Worker-based sync service, connect your devices to it, and use the same sync account on each device.

Can I export and back up my data?+

Yes. The app supports encrypted local backups and report exports in PDF, XLSX, and TXT. Optional Telegram and Google Drive delivery uses your own backend.
